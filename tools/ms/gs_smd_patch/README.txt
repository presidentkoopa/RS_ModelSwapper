GoldenSouls_SMD_Enemies.pk3 -- Super Mario Doom enemies and a high jump for Doom: The Golden Souls 1 and 2.
Built by RS_ModelSwapper tools/ms/make_gs_smd_patch.py (PLAN M4). Docs: docs/sets/golden_souls_smd_enemies.md.

LOAD ORDER: Golden Souls 1 or 2 -> GoldenSouls_SMD_Enemies.pk3. GoldenSouls1_MS.pk3 (the ModelSwapper weapon set)
may go before or after it: the two share no lump.

WHAT: the stock Doom monsters Golden Souls places (Imp, Demon, Cacodemon, Hell Knight, Baron, Revenant, Mancubus,
Arachnotron, Pain Elemental + Lost Souls, Spider Mastermind, Cyberdemon, and their Spectre / zombie / SS kin if spawned)
are drawn and voiced as Super Mario Doom's Shy Guy, Goomba, Lakitu, Blue / Red Magikoopa, Evil Luigi, Wario,
Evil Mario, Big Boo / Boo, Donkey Kong, Bowser, Koopa Troopas, Waluigi. Options > "Golden Souls: Mario enemies + jump"
has the jump on/off and its height (server cvars smd_highjump, smd_jumpz).
