Super Mario Doom enemy assets (sprites and sounds) in GoldenSouls_SMD_Enemies.pk3

Taken unchanged from "00_SMD2_00_SuperMarioDoom_Assets_SDMLITE.pk3" (the Super Mario Doom asset pack, "LITE" cut),
supplied by the pack's owner on 2026-11-08. The pack carries no author or licence text; this file records what is
known. Credit for the pixel art and the sound work belongs to the Super Mario Doom mod's makers. Mario, Luigi, Wario,
Waluigi, Bowser, Donkey Kong, Goomba, Koopa Troopa, Shy Guy, Lakitu, Magikoopa, Boo and the related sounds are
Nintendo's characters and property; this is a fan patch for personal play, distributed free, no ownership claimed.

What is here (sources/smd_lite, PROVENANCE.md has the numbers): the sprites of the 17 Doom monsters as the pack
redraws them, the projectiles those monsters fire, the monster sound lumps, and the monster lines of the pack's
SNDINFO and LANGUAGE. Nothing else of the pack (player, weapons, items, textures, flats, music, maps) is included.

The high jump code (zscript/smd_highjump.zs) is RS_ModelSwapper's (presidentkoopa), MIT like the rest of the repo.
