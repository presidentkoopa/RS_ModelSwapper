# Virtual Punch-Out (virtual-punch-out) -- Ermac; one mesh

The whole mod is a boxing glove: `models/glove/glove.md3`, one surface, 5 punch frames (34 units of swing), drawn in BOTH hands by
zscript/po_weapons.txt (main + off-hand). Nothing moves, nothing reloads: melee. The game content is Punchout.wad.
Carded as `glove/` for completeness (a seventh hand-ish mesh: a glove, no fingers).
