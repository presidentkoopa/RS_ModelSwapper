# Melee, hit effects and the voxel pack — owner's notes, 2026-10-24

Written down at the owner's request while the Quest reload work was in progress.
Nothing here is built. This is the brief for when melee comes up.

---

## 1. The melee moves wanted

Three distinct things, not one:

- **One-handed pistol whip.** A sidearm swung with the gun still in the hand.
  The gun keeps its own model; the strike is the hand's motion, not an
  animation.
- **Two-handed butt strike.** Hitting with the stock or receiver of a long gun —
  rifle, shotgun, SMG. Two hands on the weapon, a short shove forward.
- **A quick melee move**, the kind in Left 4 Dead or a modern shooter: a fast
  shove/bash on its own input that does not change weapons, does not interrupt
  the gun for long, and is always available.

The third is the one that sets the feel. It is a *move*, not a weapon switch.

---

## 2. Hit effects — use what we already have

`RS_Ballistics` and `RS_CombatFX` already carry the impact art. `RS_CombatFX`
is catalogued: **142 effects, 1120 frames, 19 categories** (`CATALOG.md`,
generated by `tools/build_catalog.py` — do not hand-edit it).

### Hard rule from the owner

**Do not reach for the big hit flash again by default.** Stated 2026-10-24, and
not for the first time. Find several candidates and let the effect fit the
surface being hit — that is the `grounded combat effects` rule, realism over a
light show.

**The flash is `RSH0`, and the problem is not that it looks bad — it is already
taken.** The owner uses it for a headshot in a headshot mod (stated 2026-10-24).
Reusing it for a melee impact would tell the player they landed a headshot when
they clubbed something with a rifle butt. That is a semantics clash, not a taste
one, which is why it cannot simply be left as the default.

**Two fixes, and the owner wants both:**

1. **Recolour it** so melee does not read as a headshot. No new art is needed —
   these are additive sprites and a translation does it. There is precedent in
   `RS_VR_Weapons/TRNSLATE.txt`, which recolours the Unmaker three ways with the
   desaturate-and-colourise form:

   ```
   WM_UnmakerWhiteHot = "0:255=%[0.60,0.30,0.10]:[2.00,1.90,1.60]"
   WM_UnmakerOrange   = "0:255=%[0.25,0.04,0.00]:[2.00,1.15,0.30]"
   WM_UnmakerRed      = "0:255=%[0.08,0.00,0.00]:[1.70,0.28,0.05]"
   ```

   One more entry in that form gives the melee flash its own colour.

2. **Let the player pick** in an options menu. Taste varies and the whole
   fifteen below are reasonable choices; the menu is cheap and it means nobody
   has to be right about the default.

### The 15 picks

Weighted toward frame count — a two-frame effect has nothing to read as motion.

(`melee_fx_contact_1/2/3.png`), composited on black, which is how an additive
sprite actually reads.

Spread: 5 smoke, 4 explosions, 2 lightning, 2 blast, 1 puff, 1 spark, 1 flash.
Everything is 7 frames or more.

**A sixteenth, usable but unviewable:** `RSF7` (flares, 18 frames, 265x249).
The catalog's File integrity table marks it `patch-data-named-png` — a raw Doom
patch with a `.png` extension on all 18 frames. GZDoom loads it fine; no strict
image reader will open it, so it could not be put on a contact sheet. Worth a
look in game.

Also flagged by that same table and worth knowing before anyone trusts their
sprite offsets: `BLUD` (3 of 7 frames), `ICEY`, `RSC3`, `RSC4`, `JGND` and
`RSR4` carry `bad-crc:grAb`, meaning the sprite offset chunk was edited without
recomputing its checksum. The offsets themselves are the thing to re-check.

### The trap when previewing these

**Many frames are not PNGs.** Of these fifteen, `JSMO` (18), `RSK3` (12),
`RSU5` (10) and `JXPL` (8) have *no* PNG frames at all — they are raw Doom lumps,
some with a `.lmp` extension and some with none. `RSS3` is 6 lumps and 1 PNG,
`RSK4` is 23 PNG and 3 lumps. GZDoom reads all of it; no image library will.

A first attempt at contact sheets globbed `*.png` and silently produced four
completely empty rows and a 1-frame RSS3, which is what the owner spotted. The
fix is to read `sheets/<id>.png`, which `tools/build_catalog.py` has already
decoded, and crop the frames out of that grid. Do not glob the category folders.

The pick should key off what was hit, the same way the ballistics surfaces
already do (`RS_Ballistics/SURFACES.txt`).

## 3. The voxel pack — optional, never required

The owner has a comprehensive ZDoom voxel pack. Brutal Doom's Quest set takes a
hard dependency on its voxel pack loading; **we should not.**

- The approach BD takes is fine to copy; the dependency is not.
- ModelSwapper must **detect** whether the voxel pack is present and use it when
  it is, and behave normally when it is not. Nothing should fail to load, and no
  weapon should end up without a model, because a pack is absent.
- There is precedent for a voxel model set in the archetype registry — rows like
  `pistol|voxel|PISG`, `shotgun|voxel|SHTG`, `chainsaw|voxel|SAWG` already
  existed as a named set alongside `vr`, so the set slot is the right home.
- The Quest weapon-pack runtime already has `rsq/voxels.zs` and
  `rsq/voxtable.zs`, plus a `MODELDEF.rsqvox` with 233 blocks — worth reading
  before writing anything new, but its table is per-gun and keyed to that pack's
  own classes.

This is the same shape as the rest of ModelSwapper: detect what is loaded, adapt,
require nothing.
